1. Request Permanent Account & Cloud Data Deletion

If you signed in to Crossing Arrows using Google Sign-In, you can request permanent deletion of your Firebase Authentication account and all associated cloud-synced gameplay data by following these steps:

  1. Send an email from the Google account email address you used in Crossing Arrows to pratikrandhir888@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Crossing Arrows — Account & Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your signed-in Google email address (and optional display name shown in the app) so we can locate and verify your account.
  4. We will verify your request, permanently delete your account and cloud records, and send a confirmation email within 30 days.

2. Delete Gameplay Progress Directly Inside the App (No Account Deletion Required)

If you only want to erase your puzzle progress, stars, and daily streaks without deleting your Google Sign-In link:

  1. Open Crossing Arrows on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) icon in the top-right corner of the Home screen.
  3. Tap Reset Progress and confirm.

This immediately erases your completed stages, star counts, and daily challenge streaks on your device (and resets your synced cloud progress if signed in).

3. Delete Local Guest Mode Data & Ad Identifiers

  • Guest Mode Players (No Sign-In): Crossing Arrows stores guest progress exclusively on your device. No account or personal data is stored on our servers. To delete all local data, either use Settings → Reset Progress in the app, clear app storage under Android Settings → Apps → Crossing Arrows → Storage & cache → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Google AdMob Advertising ID: To delete or reset your device Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads, go to Android Settings → Google → Ads on your device.
